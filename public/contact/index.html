DENTAL CARE IN CHENNAI

General Dentistry

Dental check-ups, cleaning and preventive care for healthy teeth and gums.

A check-up is a starting point for understanding your oral health and planning care. It is also a chance to raise concerns about sensitivity, bleeding gums or changes you have noticed.

What to discuss at your consultation

  • Dental examination and a discussion of your concerns
  • Cleaning and oral hygiene advice when appropriate
  • Guidance on suitable follow-up appointments

Questions to bring

Bring previous dental records and a list of medicines you take. Ask how often you should return based on your individual needs.

DENTAL CARE IN CHENNAI

Root Canal Treatment

Treatment for infected or damaged teeth, with a focus on preserving your natural tooth.

An examination helps establish the condition of the tooth and whether root canal treatment is suitable. Your dentist can discuss pain management, the treatment stages and any restoration needed afterwards.

What to discuss at your consultation

  • Assessment of the tooth and suitable treatment options
  • Explanation of the proposed care and pain management
  • A discussion of restoration and follow-up needs

Questions to bring

Ask about alternatives, the expected number of visits, the full estimate and whether a crown may be recommended.

DENTAL CARE IN CHENNAI

Dental Implants

Explore options for replacing missing teeth after an assessment of your oral health.

Implants are one option for replacing missing teeth. Suitability depends on your oral health, medical history and the condition of the supporting bone. A consultation helps compare implants with other replacement options.

What to discuss at your consultation

  • Assessment of your tooth replacement needs
  • Discussion of implants, bridges and other suitable options
  • Explanation of treatment stages and ongoing maintenance

Questions to bring

Ask about suitability, alternatives, the treatment timeline, maintenance and what is included in the estimate.

DENTAL CARE IN CHENNAI

Braces & Clear Aligners

Orthodontic options to improve tooth alignment and bite, planned around your needs.

Orthodontic care considers the position of your teeth and your bite. A consultation helps establish whether braces, clear aligners or another approach may be suitable.

What to discuss at your consultation

  • Assessment of tooth alignment and bite
  • Comparison of suitable orthodontic options
  • A discussion of follow-up appointments and retainers

Questions to bring

Ask about the proposed timeline, your role during treatment, the need for retainers and the estimated fees.

DENTAL CARE IN CHENNAI

Fillings & Crowns

Repair options for damaged teeth, with attention to everyday comfort and function.

The right restoration depends on the condition and amount of tooth structure remaining. Your dentist can explain when a filling, crown or another option may be appropriate.

What to discuss at your consultation

  • Assessment of the tooth and existing restorations
  • Discussion of appropriate repair options and materials
  • Guidance on caring for the restored tooth

Questions to bring

Ask why the proposed restoration is recommended, how to maintain it and what alternatives are available.

DENTAL CARE IN CHENNAI

Smile Consultations

Discuss whitening, appearance and the changes you have in mind for your smile.

A smile consultation starts with what you would like to change and an assessment of your dental health. The dentist can explain suitable options, their limitations and the maintenance they require.

What to discuss at your consultation

  • A discussion of your smile goals
  • Assessment of dental health before cosmetic care
  • An explanation of realistic options and maintenance

Questions to bring

Ask about expected outcomes, limitations, aftercare and whether other dental concerns should be treated first.
